# Workbench Overview

The workbench is intended to serve as a modern operational interface for high-signal decision-making.

## Primary modes

### Research mode
For analysis, evidence capture, and technical investigation.

### Financial intelligence mode
For market structure analysis, watchlists, signal synthesis, and strategy evaluation.

### Simulation mode
For paper trading and scenario testing without real-market risk.

### Human review mode
For approval, evidence comparison, and policy checks.

## Workbench outputs

- structured findings
- risk summaries
- agent task reports
- market intelligence briefs
- signal evaluations
- execution proposals
- evidence bundles

## Quality bar

The system should never confuse:
- observation with interpretation
- inference with fact
- paper simulation with live execution
- agent suggestion with approved action

## Success criteria

A useful workbench makes technical investigation and market intelligence visible, reviewable, and bounded.
